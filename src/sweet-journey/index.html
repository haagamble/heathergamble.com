🇹🇷

Turkey

Where Anna's story began

Anna was born in Turkey and lived there for several years as a young child.

Represented by Busem Milk Caramel.

🇬🇧

England

Where Jamie's story began

Jamie was born in Buxton, England, and was there for a few weeks before heading to Tajikistan.

Represented by Cadbury Heroes.

🇹🇯

Tajikistan

Jamie's childhood home

Jamie grew up in Tajikistan, with trips back to the US about every 3 years.

Represented by Fizzy Boom. (Fizzy Boom is made in Ukraine, but popular in Tajikistan.)

🇫🇷

France

Anna's early teenage years

Anna lived in France and attended ninth and tenth grade there.

Represented by Carambar.

🇩🇪

Germany

Where they met

Jamie and Anna both attended boarding school in Germany. Anna was there for eleventh and twelfth grade, while Jamie attended from ninth through twelfth grade.

Represented by Haribo Gummy Bears.

🇮🇹

Italy

Where the conversation began

Jamie and Anna traveled to Italy on their senior class trip. It was there that they began talking to each other, and becoming friends.

Represented by Limoncini candy.

🇺🇸

United States

Where the story continues

The United States is Jamie and Anna's passport country. Anna lived in the US between her years in Turkey and her years in France. It is also where Jamie and Anna are attending college, and where they are getting married.

Represented by M&Ms.

Jamie and Anna's sweet journey continues in the Carolinas. We hope that their years together are filled with love, adventure, and joy.
