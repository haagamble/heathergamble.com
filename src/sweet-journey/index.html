Jamie and Anna's sweet journey

Seven Countries. One Story.

♡

Each treat on the table represented a country in Jamie and Anna's story. Here is how those seven places helped shape the journey that brought them to Charlotte.

Discover the seven stops ↓
🇬🇧

England

Where Jamie's story began

Jamie was born in England. It is the first country in his story and the reason British chocolate belongs on the table.

Represented by Cadbury Heroes.

🇺🇸

United States

Where Anna's story began

Anna was born in the United States. It is also where Jamie and Anna are attending college and where they are beginning married life.

Represented by their personalized M&Ms.

🇹🇯

Tajikistan

Jamie's childhood home

Jamie spent much of his childhood in Tajikistan. Fizzy Boom was one of the treats he enjoyed while growing up there.

Fizzy Boom is made in Ukraine, but it represents Tajikistan because that is where it became part of Jamie's childhood memories.

🇹🇷

Turkey

One of Anna's early homes

Anna lived in Turkey for several years as a young child. It became one of the first international chapters in her story.

Represented by Busem Milk Caramel.

🇫🇷

France

Anna's high school years

Anna lived in France and attended ninth and tenth grade there. Those years became an important part of the journey that eventually led her to Germany.

Represented by Carambar.

🇩🇪

Germany

Where their paths came together

Jamie and Anna both attended boarding school in Germany. Anna was there for eleventh and twelfth grade, while Jamie attended from ninth through twelfth grade.

Represented by Haribo Goldbears.

🇮🇹

Italy

Where the conversation began

Jamie and Anna traveled to Italy on their senior trip. It was there that they began talking to each other, and a new part of their story quietly began.

Represented by Limoncini candy.

CLT

Destination: Charlotte

Seven countries helped shape their story. Charlotte is where those journeys meet as Jamie and Anna get married and begin their next chapter together.

Thank you for celebrating with Jamie and Anna and for being part of the next chapter in their story.
